Support

Every gift pays for the concerts we present across Los Angeles and for the young musicians who play them. Admission to what we present is free, which means the audience never pays at the door — our donors do.

Give now

A gift of any size goes directly to programming. LAVS Foundation is a 501(c)(3) public charity and contributions are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law.

Make a gift How we are governed

Gifts are processed by PayPal. You may give with a PayPal account, with Venmo, or with any debit or credit card — no account is needed. Monthly and annual giving are available on the same page. You will receive a receipt by email immediately.

What your gift does

Concerts that cost nothing to attend

We present chamber concerts in Los Angeles and help bring concerts by partner institutions to venues around the city. Keeping admission free is a deliberate choice: it removes the one barrier that keeps people out of a room where music is being played well.

Young musicians, early

Support for talented players from challenging financial backgrounds — the years when an instrument, a lesson or the airfare to an audition decides whether a career begins at all.

Los Angeles and Seoul

Placing musicians from the city’s conservatories as soloists with the Seocho Philharmonia in Seoul, with reciprocal appearances for Seoul-based artists here.
